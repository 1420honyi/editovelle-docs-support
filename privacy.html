隱私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · 適用於 Editovelle Docs

文件與簽名

我們在裝置上開啟、處理及輸出文件，並在本機處理簽名照片、去背與筆跡。這些內容不會為了轉檔、預覽、編輯或驗證而上傳至我們或文書引擎的伺服器。常用簽名、草稿與語言設定保存在 App 的私人儲存空間。您選擇的另存副本則保存在所指定的位置。

只有在您選擇分享或外部儲存服務時，系統才會依您的操作將所選副本交給該服務。手機系統的備份、雲端檔案供應者及分享服務依其各自設定與隱私政策運作。

相機與照片

相機用於拍攝簽名，照片選取用於匯入您選擇的圖片。您可以拒絕或在系統設定撤銷權限；手寫簽名與其他不需該權限的文書操作仍可使用。

廣告

App 使用 Google AdMob 插頁廣告。廣告 SDK 可能處理 IP 位址（可推估大致地區）、廣告或裝置識別碼、廣告互動與診斷資料，用於提供廣告、分析及防詐。請求採用非個人化廣告；非個人化並不代表完全不處理資料。可用的同意與隱私選項依地區、系統與廣告服務而異。

請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。傳送中的廣告 SDK 資料使用 TLS。您也可使用系統提供的廣告識別碼控制項。

去廣告訂閱與試用

訂閱僅在相應版本及地區啟用後提供。目前 Android 訂閱直接使用 Google Play；付款由 Google 處理，App 不取得信用卡號。驗證服務啟用後，App 會傳送 Google 購買 token、隨機安裝識別碼及必要連線資料至我們的 HTTPS 權益服務，由服務向 Google 核對並確認交易。此流程不使用 RevenueCat，也不傳送文件、簽名照片、姓名或電子郵件。

驗證服務不將原始購買 token 寫入應用資料庫或一般應用日誌；Google 處理必要交易紀錄，主機供應者可能保留 IP 位址、時間與請求狀態等連線紀錄。簽章權益回條保存在 App 私人儲存空間，支援有效期間內的離線去廣告；離線時退款或狀態變更需重新連線驗證或待回條到期才會反映。您可在 Google Play 管理或取消訂閱。iOS 訂閱目前尚未啟用。

Android 0.4.5 起，固定禮包碼完全在裝置上兌換，不傳送代碼或安裝識別碼至禮包服務。兌換紀錄、到期日及隨機安裝識別碼存於 App 私人儲存空間；更新保留舊版有效去廣告權益。禮包碼僅提供限時或永久去廣告，不扣款、不建立訂閱、不自動續訂。iOS 不提供自訂禮包碼，目前亦未啟用官方優惠碼兌換。

保留、刪除與選擇

App 不需要建立文件工具帳戶。您可在 App 中修改常用簽名，或由系統移除 App 及本機資料。外部另存的副本和系統備份需在各自位置管理；移除 App 不會取消商店訂閱，請至商店管理。商店、廣告與權益服務按其政策保留資料及必要交易紀錄。隱私或資料刪除請求可寄至 AISpace1420@gmail.com；請勿在公開留言貼上文件、簽名、收據或私人資料。

支援網站與聯絡

本網站是 GitHub Pages 靜態頁面，沒有自行加入分析追蹤或廣告。網站託管與支援留言由 GitHub 處理，適用 GitHub 隱私聲明。本 App 並非專為兒童設計。政策變更會在本頁更新日期。

Privacy policy

Updated October 19, 2026 · Editovelle Docs

Documents and signatures

Document viewing, processing, and export happen on your device. Signature photos, background removal, and handwriting are also processed locally. We do not upload this content to our servers or the document engine's servers for conversion, previews, editing, or verification. Saved signatures, drafts, and language settings use the app's private storage. Exported copies go to the location you choose.

If you choose a share destination or external storage provider, the system gives that service the selected copy at your request. Operating-system backups, cloud file providers, and sharing services operate under their own settings and policies.

Camera and photos

The camera captures signature photos; the photo picker imports images you select. You can refuse or revoke permission in system settings. Handwritten signatures and other tools that do not need that permission remain available.

Advertising

Google AdMob provides interstitial ads. Its SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate a general region), advertising or device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Consent and privacy controls depend on your region, system, and the ad service. Ad SDK data uses TLS in transit. See Google's privacy policy and your system's advertising identifier controls.

Ad-free subscriptions and trials

Subscriptions are offered only when enabled for the relevant version and region. Android subscriptions use Google Play directly. Google processes payment; the app does not receive card numbers. When verification is configured, the app sends a Google purchase token, a random installation identifier, and necessary connection data to our HTTPS entitlement service, which verifies and acknowledges the transaction with Google. This flow does not use RevenueCat or send documents, signature photos, names, or email addresses.

The service does not write raw purchase tokens to an application database or ordinary application logs. Google handles necessary transaction records, and the hosting provider may retain connection records such as IP addresses, times, and request status. Signed access receipts stay in the app's private storage for offline ad-free access during their validity. Offline refunds or status changes take effect after online verification or receipt expiry. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. iOS subscriptions are not currently enabled.

Starting with Android version 0.4.5, fixed gift codes are redeemed entirely on your device. Gift redemption does not send the code or installation identifier to a gift service. Redemption history, expiry dates, and a random installation identifier stay in private app storage; updates preserve valid access from previous versions. Gifts only provide timed or permanent ad-free access, with no charge, subscription, or automatic renewal. iOS has no custom gift codes, and official offer-code redemption is not currently enabled.

Retention, deletion, and choices

No app account is required for document tools. You can change saved signatures or remove the app and its local data through your system. Manage exported copies and backups at their respective locations. Uninstalling does not cancel a store subscription; manage it in the store. Stores, ad providers, and entitlement services retain information under their policies and applicable transaction requirements. Email AISpace1420@gmail.com with privacy or data deletion requests. Do not post documents, signatures, receipts, or personal details in a public issue.

Website and contact

This static GitHub Pages site has no added analytics or advertising. GitHub handles hosting and support issues under its privacy statement. The app is not directed specifically at children. Changes to this policy will update the date above.
